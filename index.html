Lock Screen

How do I show the countdown on my Lock Screen?

Open the app, scroll down and tap Show on Lock Screen, then lock your iPhone. The live countdown appears at the bottom of the Lock Screen.

You can change its style, size and alignment in Settings › Style & Layout.

Why does the live countdown disappear after a few hours?

iOS shows a Live Activity for up to 8 hours, then keeps it on the Lock Screen for up to 4 more before removing it. This is a system limit for every app.

That's why it's designed for the final day: with Live Countdown on the Day turned on, it starts when you open the app on the last day, and you'll get a reminder 8 hours before. Any other time, tap Show on Lock Screen again to restart it.

The button says “Turn On Live Activities”.

Live Activities were turned off for the app, usually by tapping Don't Allow on the Lock Screen. Tap the button to open Settings, turn on Live Activities, then come back to the app.

Why are the seconds hidden when my iPhone is dimmed?

On iPhones with an always-on display, iOS pauses seconds while the screen is dimmed to save battery. The countdown shows days, hours and minutes in that state, and the seconds return as soon as you wake your iPhone.

It says “Open the app to refresh”.

The live countdown passed midnight and needs the app to update the day count. Open the app and it updates straight away.

Widgets

How do I add a widget?
  1. Home Screen: touch and hold an empty area until the apps jiggle, tap Edit › Add Widget, search for Forevermore, and choose small, medium or large.
  2. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize › Lock Screen, tap the widget area under the clock, and choose Forevermore.
Why doesn't my Lock Screen widget show my colours?

iOS draws all Lock Screen widgets in its own single tint so they match the clock. Your colours and fonts appear on Home Screen widgets and on the live countdown.

A widget isn't updating.

Open the app once; widgets refresh whenever you change something. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again.

Wallpaper

How do I change my wallpaper automatically on the day?

Apple doesn't let apps change the wallpaper themselves, but the Shortcuts app can. You set it up once:

  1. In Shortcuts, open Automation, tap + and choose Time of Day.
  2. Set your wedding time, choose Daily and Run Immediately, then tap Next and New Blank Automation.
  3. Add Is Celebration Wallpaper Due, then an If action set to “is true”.
  4. Inside the If, add Get Celebration Wallpaper, then Set Wallpaper Photo, and pick Lock Screen, Home Screen or both.

It checks daily but changes the wallpaper only once, after the countdown ends. The same steps are in the app under Settings › Guides.

How do I save the wallpaper to Photos?

On the home screen, scroll to Wallpaper and tap Save Image, then Save Image again in the share sheet. iOS asks once for permission to add it to your photo library.

Notifications & settings

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that Show Countdown As is set to Notifications or Both in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Forevermore in the iPhone Settings app. Opening the app also refreshes the schedule.

How do I change the date, title or background?

Tap the Customize button at the top right of the home screen. Changes apply to the app, widgets and live countdown straight away.

What happens after the wedding?

On the day, the countdown shows “Today”. Afterwards, it gently counts the days you've been married.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later. The Dynamic Island view appears on iPhones that have one.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and nothing leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
